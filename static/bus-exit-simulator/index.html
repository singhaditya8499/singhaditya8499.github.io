What the model reveals

Keep the aisle moving.

The best sequence does not simply start at the front or the back. It feeds passengers into open gaps so the door remains busy without creating a standing traffic jam.

How it works

Model assumptions

  • Layout: 15 rows, four seats per row, one aisle, one front exit.
  • Getting up: aisle passengers take the chosen aisle-seat time. A window passenger waits until the adjacent aisle passenger has exited the bus, then takes the chosen window-seat time.
  • Movement: people walk in single file, cannot pass, and keep a small gap.
  • Door: one passenger crosses the door every second.
  • Strategies: change when rows are invited to stand; they do not change passenger walking speed.

This is a decision model, not a crowd-safety plan. Real unloading varies with luggage, mobility, children, and compliance.
